# Survey instrument

Fielded in Qualtrics, Fall 2024. Distributed by the University of Tampa graduate program office to
all current graduate students. Anonymous, voluntary, under five minutes.

**Q1** Which graduate program are you enrolled in? — 18 options (Accounting, Business
Administration, Criminology and Criminal Justice, Cybersecurity, Entrepreneurship, Finance,
Instructional Design and Technology, Nursing, Professional Communication, Athletic Training,
Business Analytics, Curriculum and Instruction, Educational Leadership, Exercise and Nutrition
Science, Marketing, Physician Assistant Medicine, Social and Emerging Media, Information and
Technology Management)

**Q2** What is your age? — Less than 20 / 20–25 / 26–30 / Over 30

**Q3** Are you an international student? — No / Yes

**Q4** How satisfied are you with the UT graduate program? — 5-point scale, Not satisfied to
Extremely satisfied

**Q5** How effective is this program in helping you to achieve your career goals? — 5-point scale,
Not effective at all to Extremely effective

**Q6** How likely are you to recommend the UT graduate program? — 5-point scale, Not likely at all
to Extremely likely

**Q7–Q16** To what extent are you satisfied with the following in the graduate program? — each on a
5-point scale, Not satisfied to Extremely satisfied:

- Q8 Knowledge of professors
- Q9 Course variety
- Q10 Career services assistance
- Q11 Networking opportunities
- Q12 Clubs/events
- Q13 Relevance of projects/assignments and tools to career goals
- Q14 Availability/flexibility of courses
- Q15 Employment opportunities
- Q16 Attractiveness of degree to potential employers

**Q17** Rank these graduate program factors from most to least important — nine items, scored
1–9.

**Q18** Please share any recommendations or comments you have for the UT graduate program. —
open-ended

## Variable roles in the analysis

- **Independent:** graduate program (Q1), age band (Q2), international status (Q3), the nine
  satisfaction measures (Q8–Q16), importance ranking (Q17).
- **Dependent:** overall satisfaction (Q4), career-goal alignment (Q5), likelihood to recommend
  (Q6).
